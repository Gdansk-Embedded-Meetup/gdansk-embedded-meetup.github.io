## O sobie
Arnas is a Senior Field Applications Engineer (Senior FAE) at Avnet-EBV, specializing in Cybersecurity and Digital Processing. In his current role, he helps customers successfully adopt, integrate, and deploy complex security and digital technologies in real-world embedded products. He acts as a technical bridge between customers, sales teams, and semiconductor vendors—providing hands-on design support while feeding real-world insights back into product and solution development.

Arnas began his career during his bachelor’s studies as a laboratory and project engineer at Kaunas University of Technology, working on ultrasonic research. He later transitioned into the private sector, where he has accumulated 15+ years of experience in embedded hardware and software design, development, and manufacturing—progressing from senior engineer roles to CTO.

Throughout his career in EBV, Arnas has contributed to the delivery of commercially successful embedded products across automotive, industrial, IoT, and telematics markets worldwide. He brings strong full-stack embedded development expertise, combined with hardware design and system-level architecture skills, with a particular focus on secure product design.
